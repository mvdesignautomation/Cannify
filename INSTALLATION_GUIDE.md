# Cannify - Android App Installation Guide

This guide explains how to download and install the **Cannify (`Cannify.apk`)** debug application onto an Android phone or tablet from Google Drive.

---

## 📱 Quick Requirements

- **Device:** Android phone or tablet (Android 8.0 / API 26 or newer recommended)
- **File:** `Cannify.apk` (shared via Google Drive link)
- **Account:** None required! Cannify works offline and keeps all data locally on your device.

---

## 🚀 Step-by-Step Installation Instructions

### Step 1: Download `Cannify.apk` from Google Drive
1. Open the **Google Drive link** on your Android device (or open the Google Drive app).
2. Locate the file named **`Cannify.apk`**.
3. Tap the **three dots (`⋮`)** next to the file or open it, then select **Download**.
4. Wait for the download to complete.

---

### Step 2: Enable "Install Unknown Apps" (If Prompted)
Because `Cannify.apk` is downloaded directly rather than from the Google Play Store, Android will ask for permission to install it.

1. When you tap the downloaded `Cannify.apk` file, you may see a message:
   > *"For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source."*
2. Tap **Settings** on the prompt.
3. Toggle ON **"Allow from this source"** (for Drive, Chrome, or My Files/Downloads).
4. Tap the **Back arrow** to return to the installer.

---

### Step 3: Install the App
1. Tap **Install** at the bottom right of the screen.
2. If Google Play Protect shows a popup stating:
   > *"Unrecognized app / Blocked by Play Protect"*
   - Tap **"Install anyway"** or **"More details" ➔ "Install anyway"**.
   - *(Note: This prompt appears because this is a developer debug APK that has not yet been published to the Google Play Store).*
3. Wait a few seconds for installation to finish.
4. Tap **Open** to launch Cannify!

---

## 🔒 Permissions & Privacy

- **No Account Required:** You do not need to log in or create an account.
- **Offline First:** Your inventory, food batches, QR codes, and history stay safely stored directly on your phone.
- **Notification Permission:** When prompted, allow notifications if you want Cannify to remind you before food items reach their best-before dates.
- **Camera Permission:** Required when using the in-app QR scanner to quickly consume or add batches to items.

---

## ❓ Troubleshooting & FAQs

### Problem: "App not installed" error
- **Reason:** An older version of Cannify might be installed on your phone with a different key.
- **Fix:** Uninstall any previous version of Cannify from your phone, then tap `Cannify.apk` to install again.

### Problem: I can't find the downloaded file
- **Fix:** Open the **Files** or **Downloads** app on your phone, go to the **Downloads** folder, and tap `Cannify.apk`.

### Problem: Play Protect blocks installation
- **Fix:** Tap **"More details"** on the Play Protect warning, then tap **"Install anyway"**.

---

## 💡 Summary of Core Features to Try
1. **Add Food Item:** Tap `+` to add items (e.g., canned sauce, frozen fruits).
2. **Add Batches:** Add multiple batches with production and best-before dates.
3. **Consume:** Use the **Consume** panel to automatically consume items using FIFO (oldest batch first).
4. **QR Codes:** View an item's QR code or use the in-app **Scan** tab to scan item QR codes.
5. **History & Restoration:** View all actions in **History** or restore accidentally deleted items in **Recently Deleted**.
